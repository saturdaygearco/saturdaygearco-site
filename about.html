What we do

We publish practical gear guides for garages, yards, pools, outdoor cooking, trucks and weekends outside. We care about what a product does, what it costs, where the compromises are and whether the features make sense for the job.

We are building Saturday Gear Co. as an independent digital publication. When we have not personally tested a product, we say so and base coverage on documented specifications, reputable independent sources and clearly identified research.

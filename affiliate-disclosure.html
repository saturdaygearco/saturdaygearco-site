Affiliate Disclosure

How Saturday Gear Co. can earn money while keeping the buying advice useful.

The short version: Saturday Gear Co. may use affiliate links. If you buy something through an eligible affiliate link, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

What an affiliate link is

An affiliate link is a trackable link to a retailer, manufacturer or other seller. When a qualifying purchase is made after using one of those links, the seller may pay Saturday Gear Co. a commission. The price you pay is generally not increased because the link is an affiliate link.

How this affects our recommendations

Affiliate availability does not determine whether a product deserves a recommendation. Our goal is to explain the tradeoffs that matter, identify who a product or category makes sense for, and help readers avoid buying more—or less—equipment than they actually need.

We may cover products or categories even when no affiliate relationship is available. We also may decide that a product is not a good fit despite an available commission.

Prices and availability

Retail prices, promotions, inventory and product specifications can change. Always confirm the current price, included accessories, warranty terms and specifications with the seller or manufacturer before purchasing.

Sponsored content and free products

If Saturday Gear Co. publishes sponsored content or receives a product or other material consideration that is relevant to a review or recommendation, we will disclose that relationship where readers can reasonably see it.

Our editorial approach

For more about how we evaluate gear and produce guides, read our Editorial Policy. You can also learn more about the site on our About page.

I'm Nicholas, founder and editor of Saturday Gear Co. I built the site around a simple idea: gear advice should help you make a better decision, not just push you toward the most expensive product on the page.

How I approach gear advice

Saturday Gear Co. focuses on the questions people usually have before they buy: what size they need, which specifications actually matter, what tradeoffs come with an upgrade and when spending more is unnecessary. Guides are built from product specifications, manufacturer documentation, credible reference material and practical use considerations.

A note on testing: Saturday Gear Co. distinguishes hands-on experience from research. We do not claim that a product was personally tested unless it actually was. When a recommendation is research-based, the site should make that clear.

How recommendations make money

Some pages may contain affiliate links. Saturday Gear Co. can earn a commission when a reader buys through an eligible link, at no additional cost to the reader. Affiliate compensation does not determine a product's rating or editorial conclusion.

Corrections and accountability

Gear changes, specifications change and mistakes can happen. If you spot something that needs correcting, contact the site at hello@saturdaygearco.com. Material corrections should be made when verified rather than quietly ignored.
